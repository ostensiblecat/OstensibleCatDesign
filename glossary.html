Glossary

Every key term in the Bleak Forest rules, with a one-line meaning and a link to the full rule. Terms are in alphabetical order.

A B C D E F G H I K L M N O P R S T V W

A

Absorb the Blow
Take a hit on your shield instead of adding the shield to your Armour Rating. A buckler absorbs up to 3 damage, a shield 6, a tower shield 12. See Shields and Absorb the Blow
Accepting trouble
Taking on your Complication, a Temptation, or trouble from the Goal’s lie on purpose. Each earns 1 Boon Point, up to 2 a session. See Earning Boon
Activity Check
The standard check: d20 + attribute modifier + skill rank, meeting or beating the BAM!. See Checks and Saves
Advantage and Disadvantage
Minor adds or subtracts 1 from the roll. Moderate, Major and Extreme move the BAM! 1, 2 or 3 steps (3, 6 or 9 points). Effects of the same type do not stack; different types do. See Advantage and Disadvantage
Adversary
The power that holds the world in its death in your Goal. It gains Holds as Ordeals fail and is faced at the Reckoning. See The Fated Goal
Anima
The mind attribute: emotion, perception, persuasion and resisting charms. Its modifier adds to reaction checks and sets how many followers you can keep. See Anima
Armour Rating (AR)
Reduces physical damage before it reaches Vitality. See Taking Physical Damage
At Risk
Adds 1 die to the Danger Die pool while its trigger lasts: darkness, being Immobilised, a Severe or Extreme Condition, Failing Doom, or resting without a guard. Several triggers still add only 1 die. See Danger Die

B

BAM!
Basic Activity Modifier: the number a roll must meet. Easy 10, Average 13, Difficult 16, Challenging 19, Formidable 22, Inconceivable 25. Each step is 3 points. See The Core Mechanic
Band
A group of Degrees climbed with one tier of Ordeal: Minor (1st–3rd), Moderate (4th–5th) and Major (6th–9th). See Bands and Gates
Blight
Lasting harm to a place when an Ordeal fails or its Ruin die reaches 0, rolled on the Blight table. See Failure and Blights
Boon Points
Points equal to your Doom at the start of each session. Spend 1 to reroll a d20, invoke your Drive, pass a Critical Damage check, cancel a Setback, test a Fragment, or declare a detail. Unspent Boon is lost at the session’s end. See Boon Points

C

Cantrip
A minor Occultism spell: Detect Magic, Read Magic or Mage Candle. Everyone with Occultism knows all three. See Cantrips
Cause
The hidden source of an Ordeal’s Trouble. The last Fragment reveals it, and the decisive check confronts it. See The Fated Path
Challenge
A personal trial that tests one attribute. Success Locks the attribute and grants an Identity Mark. See Challenges
Companion
A Devoted Follower or Soul Bound animal. A Companion can Support a task as a Second, but never Leads and never counts an Ordeal. See Companions as Seconds
Complication
A personal weakness, triggered at most once per Watch. When it is triggered, make a Spiritus save against Difficult (16) or act on it before the Watch ends, or accept it for 1 Boon Point. See Complication and Drive
Conditions
Harm to an attribute in tiers: Minor (Bloodied, Dizzied, Confounded), Moderate (Wounded, Concussed, Confused), Severe (Maimed, Brutalised, Traumatised) and Extreme, plus Special Conditions such as Blinded or Immobilised. See Conditions
Consumed
Doom 0. The character passes out of play as the Adversary’s instrument, and the Adversary gains a Hold. See Consumed
Corpus
The body attribute: strength, endurance, dodging and carrying. Its modifier adds to melee damage. At Corpus 0 you die. See Corpus
Corruption
The lasting mark of magic: a pool of d6s that grows from Magical Misfires and does not rest away. See Corruption
Critical Damage Check
At 0 Vitality, a Corpus save against Difficult (16). On a failure, the excess damage goes to an attribute, with a Condition. See Reaching 0 Vitality

D

Danger Die
A pool of 1d6 to 4d6 rolled when you enter a new sub-hex. Its total sets whether you meet nothing, or a Common, Uncommon or Rare encounter. One die is the Wyrd die. See Danger Die
Decisive check
The final check against an Ordeal’s Cause, at its Decisive BAM!. Each completed task gives Moderate Advantage, up to Extreme. See The decisive check
Degree
A character’s standing, from 1st to 9th, earned by completing Ordeals. Each Degree needs every Ordeal required by those below it. See The Nine Degrees
Doom
A fate track from 0 to 12 showing how much of you the Adversary has touched. It falls on failed Doom saves and rises when you finish Ordeals. See Doom
Doom save
d20 + Spiritus modifier against the Doom BAM!, which rises with the tier of Ordeal you are in. A failure costs 1 Doom and the Goal’s extra effect. See The Doom save
Doom states
Intact (10–12), Tested (7–9), Pressed (4–6), Failing (1–3) and Consumed (0). Each state has its own effect. See Doom states
Drive
What keeps you going. Once per Watch, gain Minor Advantage on a check made directly in its pursuit. See Drives

E

Echo
A trace banked when a character dies and returns; up to 3 are held at a time, and they are spent on return. See Echo Bank
Encounter
A scene on the way to a task: a fight, a hazard, a witness or a temptation. Winning all of a task’s encounters gives its check Moderate Advantage. See The Fated Path
Encumbered
Carrying 91% of your inventory slots up to full: movement 3, and you cannot run. See Encumbered

F

False Fragment
A red herring: it looks true but points to a false task or Cause, and never counts towards Prepared. Exposing one grants Insight. See Red Herrings
Fatigue
Markers gained from going without food, water or rest. Each takes an inventory slot. Above your Fatigue Ceiling you are Immobilised. See Fatigue
Focus
A specialisation of a skill in three ranks: Competent, Accomplished and Adept. Focus is the main source of Moderate, Major and Extreme Advantage. See Skill Focus
Footprint
The map area an Ordeal covers: a site and its surroundings for a Minor Ordeal, a town or district for a Moderate, a region of several Overland hexes for a Major. See Ordeal Scale
Fragment
A clue tied to one Challenge or Ordeal. An Ordeal needs one per task and one for its Cause. See The Fated Path

G

Gate
The first Degree of a band, 4th or 6th, opened by a single Ordeal of the new tier. See Bands and Gates
Goal
The single purpose of a Fated Road campaign, rolled once on 1d6. It sets the Adversary, the Ordeals and the Reckoning. See The Fated Goal
Grimoire
The book (1 slot) that holds an Occultist’s cantrips, spells, formulae and sigils. A spell, formula or sigil must be in it to be used. See Known Workings
Guided play
Narrator-led play that replaces the game’s oracles, such as Omen rolls, weather and the Setback table, with the Narrator’s judgment, while keeping what the players feel. See Guided Play

H

Heart
The last Fragment of the Reckoning: where the Adversary can be faced. It comes as a Vision at the first Full Rest after a character reaches 9th Degree. See Threads and the Heart
Hold
What the Adversary gains when a Goal Ordeal fails, on a near miss, or when a character is Consumed. Each Hold adds a task to the Reckoning, and 3 or more make Doom saves harder. See The Fated Goal

I

Identity Marks
Vows, Scars and Knots: who a character becomes through Challenges. See Locks, Scars, and Vows
Immobilised
You cannot move or act except to speak, make saves, and drop or hand over items, and attacks against you have Moderate Advantage. How it ends depends on its cause. See Immobilised
Insight
Minor Advantage on an Ordeal’s decisive check for exposing a False Fragment. Insight from several red herrings does not add up. See Red Herrings

K

Knot
A learned survival technique: a limited special action tied to a trigger. See Knot

L

Lead, Second and Support
In a task, one character, the Lead, makes the check. Seconds Support it: one success gives Minor Advantage, two or more give Moderate. See Party Size
Lean play
Optional trims for a lighter game: no weather, no item durability, no Mishap dice for extra actions, no Corruption pool, and no red herrings in Minor Ordeals. See Lean Play
League
4 km. An Overland hex is 2 Leagues. See The League

M

Magical Misfire
What a failed Spiritus skill check rolls on; it can feed Corruption. See Magical Misfire
Might
A tier of supernatural dominance for attributes above 18. It limits natural 20s and sets minimum BAM!s against the creature. See Might
Mishap dice
Strain from taking extra actions in a turn. Each extra action is harder, and a Short or Full Rest clears them. See Actions and Diminishing Returns
Morale
An enemy’s Anima save against Average (13) at 0 Vitality, or when its group takes its first casualty or loses half its number. On a failure it flees. See Morale

N

Natural 1 and natural 20
A natural 1 is always a fumble. A natural 20 is usually a critical success; Might can reduce it. See Checks and Saves
Near miss
A decisive check that fails by 1 or 2, and is not a natural 1. The Ordeal succeeds at a cost: the Adversary gains a Hold and no Doom is restored. The Reckoning has no near miss. See Near miss

O

Omen
The sign that a Prepared event begins. Roll 1d6 at the end of each Watch, +1 for each Watch waited; on 6 or more it arrives. See The Fated Path
Ordeal
A place in trouble with a hidden Cause: a chain of tasks ending in a decisive check. Ordeals come in Minor, Moderate and Major tiers. See The Fated Path

P

Parley
A 2d6 reaction roll for a creature whose attitude you do not know: Hostile, Wary, Curious, Friendly or Helpful. See Parley
Prepared
An event that holds all its true Fragments. Its next Omen begins it. See Fragments, Preparation, and Omens
Patience die
A Training Ordeal’s d6, set to 6 when training begins. It drops with each failed lesson, natural 1 or day lost; at 0 the trainer dismisses the trainee. See The lessons

R

Range bands
Close (3 m), Near (25 m), Far (100 m), Distant (300 m) and Span (500 m). See Range Bands
Reckoning
The last event of a Fated Road: five Threads and the Heart lead to the Adversary’s seat. Its decisive check is Inconceivable (25). See The Reckoning
Recursion
Return from death. Make a final Doom save against Average (13): on a success the Wyrd Sisters send you back with 1 Vitality and 1 less Doom. See Full Recursion
Ruin die
A d6 set to 6 when an Ordeal’s Omen arrives. It drops with time and with each Setback; at 0 the Trouble peaks and a Blight strikes. See Setbacks and the Ruin Die
Ruse
An opposed Anima check to bribe, deceive or steal before a fight begins. Add your Guile rank. See Ruse

S

Save
d20 + attribute modifier to resist harm, Easy (10) unless a rule says otherwise. See Saves
Scar
A permanent wound, physical, mental or spiritual. It can be reinterpreted but never removed. See Scar
Setback
Something going wrong in an Ordeal: a failed task, a natural 1, a lost encounter or a Wyrd die result. Each lowers the Ruin die by 1. See Setbacks and the Ruin Die
Short Rest and Full Rest
A Short Rest is 1 Watch: recover all Vitality, clear Mishap dice and 1 Fatigue. A Full Rest is 2 Watches: all of that, all Fatigue, and 1 point of each damaged attribute. See Rest
Skill Mishap
What a natural 1 on a Corpus or Anima check, or a failed check after your first action, rolls on. See Skill Mishap
Skill ranks
Apprentice (+1), Skilled (+2), Expert (+3), Master (+4) and Grand Master (+5), added to checks with the skill. See Skill Progression
Spiritus
The spirit attribute: casting, sigils, dealing with magical beings and Doom saves. At Spiritus 0 you fall into a coma. See Spiritus
Succeed with style
Beating the BAM! by 6 or more. Searches find exactly what you seek, attacks do maximum damage, and in an Ordeal a task raises the Ruin die by 1. See Succeed with Style

T

Taint
The sign an Ordeal’s Cause leaves on everything it touches, true and False Fragments alike. Each Taint fits three Causes, so it narrows the Cause without naming it. See Taint
Task
What must be done to put an Ordeal’s place right. An Ordeal has 2, 3 or 4, across at least two attributes. See The Fated Path
Temptation
An encounter offering an easier way at a price. Accepting it calls for a Doom save and earns 1 Boon Point. See When to make a Doom save
Thread
The Cause Fragment of one of the Goal’s own Major Ordeals. Five Threads, with the Heart, open the Reckoning. See The Fated Path
Time units
Round (6 seconds), Tick (10 minutes), Mark (1 hour), Watch (4 hours), Day (6 Watches), and a travel day of 2 Watches on the move. See Time Units
Trained
The reward of a successful Training Ordeal: at the next Degree, the next rank in the trained skill or Focus costs no skill points and may go beyond the one-rank-per-Degree limit. See Training Ordeals
Training Ordeal
A personal Ordeal with a trainer, built around one skill or Focus. Once per band it can replace one of the trainee’s Ordeals, never a gate, and the Adversary feels the time it takes. See Training Ordeals
Trouble
What is wrong in an Ordeal’s place, caused by its hidden Cause. See The Fated Path

V

Vitality
Your capacity to endure damage: 12 plus all three attribute modifiers, from 1 to 24 at the start. See Vitality
Vow
A promise or personal law: a lasting benefit paired with a constraint. Breaking it suspends the benefit. See Vow

W

Watch
4 hours, the main unit of strategic play. A day has 6 Watches. See Time Units
Working
Any single arcane effect: a spell, formula, sigil, psionic power, healing mending or True Name working. You can attempt only workings you know, but any Order. See Known Workings
Wyrd die
The odd-coloured die in the Danger Die pool. On a 1 a Special Event happens; inside an Ordeal’s footprint, roll 1d6 for a Setback, a Witness or a flare instead. See Danger Die
