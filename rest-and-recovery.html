Rest and Recovery

Rest and recovery are a necessary part of the adventuring life. How long you rest decides how much you recover. In the Dead Forest and Bleak Forest you cannot rest without setting a guard. Elsewhere, resting without a guard raises the danger (see Danger Die › Resting without a guard).

Types of Rest

Rest Time Vitality Mishap Dice Fatigue Attributes
Recovery 1 Tick (10 minutes) Recover 1 Clear 1 — —
Short Rest 1 Watch (4 hours) Recover all Clear all Clear 1 marker —
Full Rest 2 Watches (8 hours) Recover all Clear all Clear all markers Heal 1 point of each damaged attribute

Recovery

Recovery is a short pause to gather your wits and regain some composure.

  • Recover 1 Vitality per Tick (10 minutes) of undisturbed rest.
  • Each Tick of Recovery clears 1 Mishap die.

Short Rest

Short Rest is one Watch (4 hours) of minimal, undisturbed activity.

  • Recover all Vitality.
  • Clear all Mishap dice.
  • Clear 1 Fatigue marker.

Full Rest

Full Rest is two Watches (8 hours) of minimal, undisturbed activity.

  • Recover all Vitality.
  • Clear all Mishap dice.
  • Clear all Fatigue markers.
  • Heal 1 point of each damaged attribute. For example, a character who has lost both Corpus and Anima recovers 1 point of each.
  • Most spell effects, harmful or helpful, end unless noted otherwise.

Setting a Guard

The Dead and Bleak Forest

In the Dead and Bleak Forest you cannot rest without a guard.

Resting without a guard. Outside the Dead and Bleak Forest, you may rest without setting a guard. If you do, add 1 die and roll the pool each Mark instead of once. In the Dead and Bleak Forest you cannot rest without a guard.

See Danger Die for how the pool is built and rolled, and Terrain, Weather, & Danger for terrain where safe rest is not possible.

Food, Water, and Rest

End of day. If you take a Short or Full Rest this Watch, reduce your Food and Water dice by 1 pip each. A rest taken without both restores nothing.

You cannot recover Vitality or attribute points if you lack food, water, or rest. After 24 hours without food, water or rest, make a Corpus save; on a failure, gain 1 Fatigue marker. Each marker takes up one inventory slot. If you have no free slots, you must drop an item or become Immobilised until you make room. Repeat the save each Watch you remain without them.

See the End of Watch Checklist in Order of Play.

Removing Fatigue

  • An uninterrupted Short Rest of 1 Watch removes 1 Fatigue marker.
  • An uninterrupted Full Rest of 2 Watches, or 8 hours, removes all Fatigue.

Your Fatigue Ceiling equals twice your Corpus modifier, with a minimum of 2. If your Fatigue exceeds this limit, you become Immobilised.

Eating, drinking, and completing a 4-hour Short Rest end the Immobilised condition and stop the hourly saves.

Corpus lost to Fatigue recovers at a rate of 1 point per Full Rest as per normal.

See Fatigue in Conditions and Death for the full rules.

Recovering from Conditions

Recovering from Conditions depends on their severity:

  • Minor Conditions end once their duration passes or healing is applied.
  • Moderate Conditions drop to Minor once their duration passes or healing is applied.
  • Severe Conditions drop to Moderate once their duration passes or healing is applied.
  • Extreme Conditions are permanent unless magical healing is applied.

For healing that speeds this up, see the Healing Arts.

Rest in Other Rules

Rest also appears in these rules elsewhere in the Bleak Forest Engine:

  • True quiet. Nothing stirs. Your next Recovery or rest in this sub-hex restores 1 extra Vitality.
  • Respite (1st Order spell). Each casting lets the target recover 2 extra Vitality per Tick (10 minutes) of rest for the duration.
  • Vision (Fragment). A dream during rest, gained by a Short or Full Rest.
  • Corruption. The Corruption Pool never clears from rest, Short Rest, or Full Rest.
