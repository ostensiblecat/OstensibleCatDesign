Quick Start

Quick Start

Eight steps from an empty table to the first Ordeal. Each step is a reference card: Read next links to the full rules, and the card numbers point to the matching cards on the One-Page Core, which follows the same order.

1Gather what you need

  • A d20 and up to four d6. Make one d6 a different colour: it is the Wyrd die in a Danger Die pool.
  • Pencil, paper or a character sheet, and the 18 by 18 hex map if you use one.
  • Decide how you play: with a Narrator, solo, or Co-Op, and whether to trim the rules with Lean Play.
  • Online, the Die Roller, Character Generator and Ordeal Generator do the rolling for you.

Read next: Ways to Play · What You Need (One-Page Core Ways to play)

2Learn the one roll

  • Roll d20 + attribute modifier + skill rank. Meet or beat the BAM!, usually Average (13) to Formidable (22).
  • Advantage makes the BAM! lower and Disadvantage makes it higher, in Minor, Moderate, Major and Extreme steps.
  • Saves are d20 + attribute. Natural 1 fumbles; natural 20 usually succeeds.

Read next: Core Mechanic · Advantage and Disadvantage (One-Page Core card 1, card 2)

3Make a character

  1. Roll 3d6 for Corpus, Anima and Spiritus, in order.
  2. Vitality is 12 + all three modifiers.
  3. Roll your skills and Focuses, or spend 9 skill points.
  4. Roll starting gear, and roll or choose your Drive and Complication.
  5. You start at 1st Degree with Doom 6.

Read next: Character Creation · Drive and Complication · Character Generator (One-Page Core card 3)

4Set up the campaign

  1. Choose a prepared map or a discovered map.
  2. Roll the starting hex; the nearest settlement is home.
  3. Roll the Fated Goal and read its premise.
  4. Seed the first Minor Ordeal within 2 hexes, and give its first Fragment as a Rumour heard at home. The Fated Road has begun.

Read next: Starting a Campaign · Prepared or discovered map (One-Page Core card 4)

5Play a session in Watches

  1. Start: each character’s Boon Points equal their Doom.
  2. Each Watch (4 hours): look around, choose one action (Travel, Explore, Forage, Rest, Craft, a Ritual or an Ordeal task), and roll the Danger Die if the action calls for it.
  3. End the Watch: burn light, eat and drink after rest, roll for Omens, count down Ruin dice, check Doom.
  4. End of session: a Doom save if you are Tested; unspent Boon is lost.

Read next: Order of Play · End of Watch (One-Page Core card 5)

6Meet trouble

  • A Danger Die of 6+ is an encounter. Check surprise and attitude, then Parley, Ruse, Hide, Flee or fight.
  • Damage, less Armour, comes off Vitality. At 0 Vitality, a failed Critical Damage check sends the rest to an attribute as a Condition.
  • Rest heals: a Short Rest is 1 Watch, a Full Rest is 2. In the Forest, set a guard.

Read next: Actions, Encounters, & Mishaps · Conditions and Death · Rest and Recovery (One-Page Core card 6, card 7)

7Follow the Fated Road

  1. Find the Ordeal’s Fragments, one per task and one for the Cause. Watch for False Fragments.
  2. Once Prepared, wait for the Omen. When it comes, set the Ruin die to 6.
  3. Work the tasks: a Lead checks, Seconds support, and every Setback lowers the Ruin die.
  4. Make the decisive check, with Advantage for each task done. Success counts towards your next Degree; failure gives the Adversary a Hold.

Read next: Ordeal Structure · Building the road · Ordeals on the Road (One-Page Core card 8)

8Grow, and end the age

  • Doom falls when you fail and rises when Ordeals succeed. Spend Boon to reroll, cancel a Setback or declare a detail; earn it by accepting trouble.
  • Completed Ordeals raise your Degree: 5 Minor, then 6 Moderate, then 10 Major.
  • Win five Threads from the Goal’s Major Ordeals, reach 9th Degree, find the Heart, and face the Reckoning.

Read next: Doom and Boon · The Nine Degrees · The Reckoning (One-Page Core card 9, card 10)
