Loot

Loot is defined as any item that is found upon a creature, in a loot cache, or a wandering trader. This indicates how many d6 should be rolled on the Loot Die table.

Foraging

You can attempt to find food, water, Healing Herbs, or Alchemical Components. Taking time to forage is assumed to take 1 watch (4 hours) per attempt. Check the danger die table for your terrain type every hour for encounters. Check weather every watch (4 hours). The terrain type dictates the Basic Activity Modifier (BAM!) for foraging attempts. Foraging attempts are modified by the Lore skill (Wits) and Foraging Skill focus. Before rolling, declare your intent: Herb Search (seeking Healing Herbs for the Healing Arts) or Component Search (seeking Alchemical/Spell Components). Your declared intent grants Minor Advantage on finding that type when a forage table entry offers a choice. Food and water results are unaffected by intent. The table below gives Foraging results:

Forage II

Die Roll (d6)DescriptionNotes
1Healing Herbs (Common)Roll on Herb Compendium (Common tier)—yields 1 Healing Herb usable directly in Healing Arts powers. Also usable as 1d3 food rations if brewed (takes 1 hour, no roll). Cannot substitute for Alchemical Components.
2Trapped Game1d6+1 food rations. Requires 1 watch to set and check traps.
3Uncommon Herbs or ComponentsDeclare intent. Herb Search: roll on Herb Compendium (Uncommon tier) 1d2 times—each result is a usable Healing Herb. Component Search: roll on Uncommon Components Table 1d2 times. No declared intent: roll 1d2 (1 = Herb, 2 = Component), roll on appropriate table once.
4Honeycomb1d6 food rations. Also counts as 1 Tallow substitute (common component).
5Freshwater PoolRestore food and water dice to 1d6 if currently lower. Remove 1 fatigue marker.
6Uncommon Healing Herbs (Rare Find)Roll on Herb Compendium (Uncommon tier). The herbs found are usable directly as Healing Arts components. If you have the Healing Focus, gain Minor Advantage on the next Healing Arts check made using one of these herbs.

Forage III

Die Roll (d6)DescriptionNotes
1Rare Herbs or ComponentsDeclare intent. Herb Search: roll on Herb Compendium (Rare tier)—yields 1 Rare Healing Herb (Ashen Kingsfoil, Moonpetal, Silverdew, etc.). Component Search: roll on Rare Components Table once. No intent: roll 1d2 and consult appropriate table.
2Creature CacheA creature’s hidden store. 2d6 food rations and 1d3 common components.
3Alchemical BloomA rare flowering plant. Functions as a rare component. Grants Minor Advantage on the next Spiritus save (lasts 1 watch).
4Hidden SpringFully restore food and water dice. Remove all current fatigue markers.
5Spell TraceResidual magic. Gain 1 free 1st Order Spell use (no Vitality cost, no components) within the next watch.
6Fae OfferingSmall gifts left by fae: roll on Herb Compendium (Uncommon) once AND roll on Uncommon Components Table once, plus 1d6 silver coins. The fae leave both kinds of gift—they do not distinguish between healer and alchemist.
