Game

The Nameless

In Escape the Bleak Forest you play the Nameless. Each death brings Recursion: unlocked attributes are rolled again, and Echoes let you carry something through.

Life 1
Echoes 0 / 3
Locked 0 / 3
Recursion log

    1. Attributes

    Roll 3d6 for each attribute in order: Corpus, Anima, Spiritus. Do not change the scores or the order.

    2. Vitality

    Roll attributes first.

    3. Skills and Focus

    Method

    No skill above Expert (+3). A Focus needs its skill, and can't have more ranks than the skill.

    4. Starting Gear

    Everyone starts with simple clothing, a backpack, a provisions pouch (three days' rations and water), three torches, and a components pouch. These take no backpack slots. Roll on the Starting Simple Weapons table. With the Combat skill, also roll for armour, then a d6: 1–3 Martial, 4–5 Ranged, 6 both.
