One-Page Core

The whole game on one page, in the order you meet it in play. Each card is a reference card: its links and its Full rules line take you to the pages behind it, and terms are defined in the Glossary. New to the game? Start with the Quick Start, whose steps point to these cards by number.

1. Rolling

Check: d20 + attribute modifier + skill rank. Meet or beat the BAM!.

Easy 10Average 13Difficult 16
Challenging 19Formidable 22Inconceivable 25
  • Save: d20 + attribute, Easy (10) unless stated.
  • Natural 1 always fumbles. Natural 20 usually succeeds.
  • With style: beat the BAM! by 6 or more.

3. Your character

  • Corpus, Anima, Spiritus: body, mind, spirit. Corpus 0 kills; Spiritus 0 means a coma.
  • Vitality: 12 + all three modifiers.
  • Skills: +1 Apprentice to +5 Grand Master. Focus: Competent, Accomplished, Adept.
  • Drive and Complication: +1 when you pursue your Drive; resist or accept your Complication.
  • Identity Marks: Vows, Scars and Knots, earned through Challenges.

Full rules: Character Creation · Character Generator · Skills & Focus

4. Starting a campaign

  1. Choose how you play and a prepared or discovered map.
  2. Characters start at 1st Degree, Doom 6.
  3. Roll the starting hex; the nearest settlement is home.
  4. Roll the Fated Goal.
  5. Seed the first Minor Ordeal within 2 hexes. Its first Fragment arrives as a Rumour: the Fated Road begins.

Full rules: Starting a Campaign · The Road’s Map

5. A Watch: time and travel

Each Watch: look around, choose an action, roll the Danger Die if the action calls for it, deal with what comes, then end the Watch (light, food, Omens, Ruin dice, Doom).

  • Watch: 4 hours, one strategic action. A day has 6; a travel day is 2.
  • Hexes: Overland 8 km, Operational 2 km, Exploration 500 m.
  • Danger Die: 1d6 to 4d6 on entering a sub-hex. 2–5 nothing, 6–9 Common, 10–12 Uncommon, 13+ Rare. Wyrd die 1: Special Event.
  • Rest: Short (1 Watch) or Full (2 Watches). Set a guard in the Forest.

Full rules: Order of Play · Distance, Movement, & Time · Terrain & Weather

6. Encounters

  1. Distance and surprise (opposed Anima).
  2. Attitude: Parley 2d6, Hostile to Helpful.
  3. Approach: Parley, Ruse, Hide, Flee or fight.
  4. Initiative: 1d6 + Anima. Enemies check Morale at losses.

Full rules: Actions, Encounters, & Mishaps · Encounter Tables

7. Harm

  1. Damage minus Armour Rating comes off Vitality.
  2. At 0 Vitality: Critical Damage check, a Corpus save against Difficult (16). A failure sends the excess to an attribute, with a Condition.
  3. Conditions run Minor, Moderate, Severe, Extreme, by attribute.
  4. Fatigue fills inventory slots; above your Fatigue Ceiling you are Immobilised.
  5. Death: a final Doom save for Recursion.

Full rules: Conditions and Death · Rest and Recovery

8. Ordeals

  1. Find Fragments: one per task, one for the Cause. Beware False Fragments.
  2. Prepared → Omen: roll 1d6 each Watch, +1 per Watch waited; 6+ begins it. Set the Ruin die to 6.
  3. Tasks: a Lead checks, Seconds Support (one success Minor, two Moderate). Win all encounters for Moderate Advantage. Each Setback lowers the Ruin die.
  4. Decisive check: Moderate Advantage per completed task, up to Extreme.
  5. Outcome: success counts towards Degrees and restores Doom. A near miss (fail by 1–2) succeeds, but the Adversary gains a Hold. Failure brings a Blight, a Hold and −1 Doom.

Full rules: Challenges and Ordeals · The Fated Road

9. Doom and Boon

  • Doom 0–12: Intact, Tested, Pressed, Failing, Consumed.
  • Boon = Doom at session start. Spend 1 to reroll, invoke your Drive, cancel a Setback, test a Fragment, or declare a detail.
  • Earn Boon: +1 for accepting your Complication, a Temptation or the Goal’s lie (2 a session).

Full rules: Doom and Boon

Ways to play

Lean play: for a lighter game, switch off any of these; nothing else depends on them (Lean Play):

  • Weather: no weather rolls; terrain alone sets speed and danger.
  • Durability: items never wear; they break only when the story says so.
  • Mishap dice: one action per turn, no pushing. A natural 1 still rolls a Skill Mishap.
  • Corruption: a Magical Misfire’s immediate result only.
  • Red herrings: none in Minor Ordeals.

Always keep the core roll, Advantage, Conditions, the Danger Die, Fragments and Omens, Doom and Boon, and the Fated Road.

Guided play: with a Narrator, let them decide what the players never see (Omens, weather, Setbacks, reactions) and keep what they watch happen: the Ruin die, Doom and the Danger Die (Guided Play).
