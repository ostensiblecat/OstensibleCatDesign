The Adversary’s Servants

Every Fated Goal’s Adversary works through servants, and the road meets them in order: servants and the people they have fooled in Minor Ordeals, lieutenants in Moderate Ordeals, champions guarding the Threads in Major Ordeals, and the Adversary itself at the Reckoning (see The Adversary). This page shows how to build them, gives ready stat blocks for the mortal servants the Compendium does not cover, lists who serves each Goal, and sets out what they carry.

Monsters, spirits and the dead come from the Creature Compendium. Stat blocks here use the same fields, read the same way (see Reading a Stat Block and Building Rules).

Building a Servant

Build any servant in four steps. Its rank sets its power; its base sets its shape; its Court sets its flavour.

  1. Choose its rank from the Ordeal it serves, on the table below.
  2. Choose a base. Use a stat block on this page for people, or a Compendium creature for anything else. The rosters below suggest a base for every named servant.
  3. Fit the base to the rank. If the base’s Might or BAM! does not match its rank, raise or lower it (see Raising and lowering).
  4. Add the Court’s gift at its rank’s strength, and give it its Court’s token and its Loot Dice.

The key attribute is the one the servant leads with: Corpus for a fighter, Spiritus for a worker of magic, Anima for a schemer. Its Might comes from that attribute (Might = score − 18). Scale the number appearing by party size as usual (see Party Size).

Raising and lowering

  • To raise a base one rank, raise its key attribute to the new rank’s norm, which gives it Might, and add one level of Toughness. Recalculate its Vitality and damage, set its BAM! to at least the rank’s minimum, and add the Court’s gift and Loot Dice of the new rank. A raised champion also gains Dread presence and Thread-keeper (as the Champion, Warlord).
  • To lower a base one rank, reverse this: lower its key attribute to the new rank’s norm, remove one level of Toughness, and set its BAM! to the lower rank’s. It keeps its nature and traits, but the lower rank’s gift.
  • A Minor Ordeal’s Cause that names a creature stronger than a servant, such as a Lesser demon in a well, is that creature lowered to servant rank. It is the Trouble’s heart, but not yet a power.

Thread-keeper

A champion guards one of the Goal’s Threads. While it holds the Thread it never checks morale and cannot be bribed, charmed or turned. It fights as the Cause encounter of its Major Ordeal; beating it there gives the decisive check its Advantage, but only the decisive check ends the Ordeal.

Mortal Servants

The Compendium holds monsters, spirits and the dead; these are the people who serve the Adversary. Type Mortal has no shared traits: mortals eat, sleep and bleed, can be swayed by Guile and Parley, and can change sides. A Mortal’s nature can change with its Court: an Undying knight is Undead and a Sidhe knight is Fae. When a Court makes it so, add that type’s shared traits (see Creature Types).

Loot, Tokens and Fragments

Loot

Roll the Loot Dice for the servant’s rank, as given in its stat block or the Ranks table, and add what its base creature yields in the Compendium. A servant also carries its Court’s token.

Court tokens

Every Court Agent, lieutenant and champion carries a token of its Court. A token shows its Goal’s signature Taint, so it proves a Fragment belongs to the Goal (not that the Fragment is true). Inside an Ordeal’s footprint, a token can be the Ordeal’s Relic Fragment, if the Narrator seeds it there.

Goal Token
The Unending Autumn A chip of hearthstone, warm to the touch, that slowly rots cloth wrapped around it.
The Chained Gate An iron name-tag, stamped with a dead stranger’s name.
The Grafted Tree A graft-seed in a twist of bark, sticky with black sap.
The Held Tide A sluice-key on a green-black cord.
The Unfallen King A small life-tithe reliquary, faintly warm, with a heartbeat inside.
The Sleeping Devourer A tuning stone that hums the lullaby when held.

Fragments

  • Servants hold the Witness and Relic Fragments. When seeding an Ordeal, put its Witness Fragments in the mouths of servants and fooled folk, and its Relic Fragments in a lieutenant’s papers or a servant’s pack (see Fragment forms).
  • A servant brought to Helpful, or captured and questioned, gives up the Fragment it holds. If it holds none, it repeats its Goal’s favourite lie, which may be one of the Ordeal’s False Fragments.
  • A servant met outside any footprint, as an encounter on the road, carries word of its Goal’s next Ordeal: if that Ordeal is not yet Prepared, the servant reveals the form of its next missing Fragment, as an Omen would.
  • Each champion guards something the Reckoning needs: an object such as the Barrow-King’s crown or the Lady of Bones’ key, or a prisoner such as the Winter’s Daughter. The Reckoning task its Thread reveals names it. Take it, or free it, when the champion falls. Its Thread is its Ordeal’s Cause Fragment, gained as usual.

Holds and the Consumed

  • A Hold’s servant. When a failure gives the Adversary a Hold such as “a servant raised”, build that servant at the failed Ordeal’s rank, from the same roster. It guards the extra Reckoning task the Hold adds, as one of that task’s encounters.
  • The Consumed. A Consumed character can return as one of the Adversary’s creatures: a servant in a Minor Ordeal, a lieutenant in a Moderate Ordeal or a Thread’s guard in a Major Ordeal. Build it from the character’s own sheet: keep its attributes and skills, raise it to the rank, and add its new Court’s gift. It remembers the party.
Example: The Burning Vale (The Unending Autumn, Major 1) needs its champion, the captain of the Ember Knights. The Narrator builds a Champion, Warlord, adds all three columns of the Ember Court’s gift (Immunity to mundane fire; Elemental Affinity II (Fire) and +1 fire damage; a Burning aura and Weakness I (cold)), and gives it 1d3 Court Knights. Its token is a hearthstone chip on a chain. What it guards, the Winter’s Daughter chained in the Weeping Tower, is what the matching Reckoning task will need. When it falls, the party rolls 3 Loot Dice.
