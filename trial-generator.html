Challenges, Ordeals & the Fated Road

Generate Ordeals, Challenges and Fragments using the rules in The Fated Road. Start a Fated Road to follow one Goal from 1st Degree to the Reckoning, or roll a one-off Ordeal with the general generator. Find Fragments (and expose the red herrings), become Prepared, receive the Omen, then race the Ruin die to the decisive check. Keep events in the Ledger to track them through play. Everything is saved in this browser.

Roll the Goal Fate has set for the party. The road follows it through 3 Minor, 5 Moderate and 5 Major Ordeals to the Reckoning, tracking Degrees, Holds, Threads and each character's Doom.

A place in trouble with a hidden Cause. Each true Fragment uncovers a task, the last reveals the Cause, and False Fragments lead astray. Encounters and enemies scale to the party.
