Challenges, Ordeals & the Fated Road

Generate Ordeals, Challenges and Fragments using the rules in The Fated Road. Start a Fated Road to follow one Goal from 1st Degree to the Reckoning, or roll a one-off Ordeal with the general generator. Find Fragments (and expose the red herrings), become Prepared, receive the Omen, then race the Ruin die to the decisive check. Keep events in the Ledger to track them through play. Everything is saved in this browser.

New to the generator? Start on the Fated Road tab and follow its numbered steps; each Ordeal in the Ledger shows which step it is at.

Roll the Goal Fate has set for the party. The road follows it through 5 Minor, 6 Moderate and 10 Major Ordeals to the Reckoning, tracking Degrees, Holds, Threads and each character's Doom.

A place in trouble with a hidden Cause. Each true Fragment uncovers a task, the last reveals the Cause, and False Fragments lead astray. Encounters and enemies scale to the party.

A personal trial. It tests one attribute, and success Locks it. On the Fated Road each character faces one Challenge per band, and it can be woven into an Ordeal of the same tier.

Already Locked (rerolled if chosen)

Seek out a trainer and undergo an Ordeal built around one skill or Focus. Once per band, a successful Training Ordeal can replace one of the trainee’s Ordeals, but never the Ordeal that opens a gate. The Adversary feels the time it takes.

Roll a single Fragment, or a red herring to plant among the true ones. If you're unsure which event a found Fragment belongs to, a Lore check (Difficult 16) names it.
