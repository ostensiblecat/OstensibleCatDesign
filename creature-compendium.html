Using This Compendium

↑ Contents

This compendium lists every creature available to both games. In Escape the Bleak Forest, creatures are met through the Danger Die and the encounter tables in Playing the Game. In Hail Three Wyrd Sisters, the natural roster continues alongside the Abrikoth and their servants. Stat blocks apply unchanged in both games.

Every stat block is built from the same rules, set out below, so the Narrator can build new creatures the same way.

Reading a Stat Block

Loot What the creature yields; harvesting checks use Wits (Survival) unless stated.

Building Rules

Vitality. Vitality = 12 + Size Vitality modifier + Corpus, Anima and Spiritus modifiers (Character Rules attribute table) + Toughness. The minimum is 1. The 24-point Vitality cap applies only to player characters; creatures have no cap. A Mindless creature (Anima and Spiritus of 1) adds no Anima or Spiritus modifier. An Incorporeal creature has no Corpus and no Size modifier: its Vitality is 12 + Anima and Spiritus modifiers + Toughness.

Scores above 18. Use the Character Rules modifiers: 19–20 +5, 21–22 +6, 23 +7, 24 +8, 25 +9, and +1 for each point above 25.

Might. Might = the highest attribute norm − 18. Tier I (Imposing) is Might 1–4, Tier II (Dread) 5–7, Tier III (Primordial) 8+. Might effects apply only to the attribute that has it. The tier also sets a minimum BAM!: Difficult (16) at Tier I, Challenging (19) at Tier II, Formidable (22) at Tier III. See Might in Character Rules.

Attacks. A creature’s second and later attacks in a round follow the successive-attack steps in Playing the Game. Each level of the Multiple Attacks trait removes one step.

Recovery. Unless noted otherwise, creatures regain Vitality as characters do: 1 point per Tick of undisturbed rest.

Morale. Unless an entry says otherwise, a creature reduced to 0 Vitality makes an Anima save, Average (13), or flees. A group makes the same save when it takes its first casualty and again when it has lost half its number (Playing the Game). Giants make these saves at Moderate Advantage.

Creature Types

Animal. Creatures of the natural world. They have no innate magic and can feel but are not self-aware. Anima is usually 1–12 and dispositions are usually Wary or Curious. Animals with Anima above 1 can be influenced with Guile and can become Soul Bound.

Demonic. Malevolent spirits from hellish planes who torment, tempt and corrupt mortals and treat all other life as something to toy with and discard. All have True Names and can be summoned, banished and bound with Conjuration. Most are harmed fully only by cold iron or magic.

Draconic. Large, intelligent, highly magical reptiles. Dispositions vary, but all are avaricious: a draconic creature will negotiate if offered enough treasure (treat it as Curious at minimum unless provoked). Shared traits: Armour I or better; Immunity to mundane flame; Keen Sight. Draconic creatures with Corpus 19+ have Might.

Elemental. Sentient beings made of the fundamental elements. Emotionless and implacable once set on a course. All have True Names and can be summoned, banished and bound with Conjuration. Each has Elemental Form for its element. An Elder Elemental is one of the four great Elementals raised to Might Tier II–III (see the note under Elemental).

Fae. Quixotic beings from the fey lands, varied in form and temper but always magical, with a taste for mischief or malice. All have True Names and can be summoned, banished and bound with Conjuration. Most suffer Bane (Cold Iron).

Giant. Huge magical humanoids born of the world and of the forest’s primal nature. Shared traits: Armour I or better; Might set by Corpus; Immunity to Frightened; terrain-bound (a giant will not pursue beyond its native terrain); morale saves at Moderate Advantage.

Magical Creature. Creatures that mix natural and fantastic features. Shared traits: Armour I or II where it suits their form; at least one innate special ability. Not subject to True Name binding unless Spiritus is 12+. Can become Soul Bound if Anima is above 1 and the creature is not Hostile by nature.

Undead. Creatures animated after death, mindless or highly intelligent, corporeal or incorporeal. Shared traits: Immunity to poison, disease and Fatigue; no need for food, water or sleep; Hostile unless bound or compelled. Intelligent undead (Anima 4+) have True Names and can be bound with Conjuration. Mindless undead (Anima 1–3) cannot be affected by Guile.

Water Breathing I–II Breathes water only (I), or both water and air (II).
