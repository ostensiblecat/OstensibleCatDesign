Bleak Forest

Tabletop RPG System

The Forest is infected, and it is spreading. You must stop it. There is no other option. In a bleak world where your trials are carved in pain, darkness, and blood, the world must be made whole again. The path is brutal, and your place in its rebirth is yours to define. Will you achieve greatness, or will you fall short of glory before you are remade? .

SThe Bleak Forest is a tabletop roleplaying game system set in a grim world of dark fantasy. You are navigating an environment beset by challenges at every turn. Yeah, you’ve heard it all before – a dark scourge and a grim world where peril awaits around every corner in a doom-haunted world. Where life is short and you must manage resources and master your abilities to stave off the pain and misery around you. While that certainly is an element in this game (and a big part of the flavour), there is something a little different on offer here. The Bleak Forest game system is intended be a game of ominous positivity: Your world is failing and you will save it – there simply is no other choice. As the world falls into darkness, disease, and despair, it will eventually be reborn. Your part in that rebirth is inescapable, but the hell you must endure to get to the other side is defined by your actions. Inspired by mythic cycles of calamity and rebirth such as the Norse tales of Ragnarok and Fimbulvetr; the Hindu cosmic cycle of Samsara; etc.; Beyond the Bleak Forest is set at the last stages of the cycle before rebirth. You are part of that cosmic cycle, though forces work against you preventing the rebirth and stalling the cycle and drawing out the anguish of a world in peril.

The basic game engine mechanic is simple: roll over a target number, apply modifiers, and determine result. Pretty standard stuff. However, one of the driving mechanical design goals is to create a game that lent itself to being able to handle different modes of play; Co-Op, GM led, and solo. To do that, it needed to be simple enough that there would not be endless lookups and modifier balancing all the while providing structure and direction for guided play through solo or Co-Op modes, but be flexible enough to provide free form GM guided play and still feel satisfying.

The core rules present the base rules for playing the game that are common across Bleak Forest Games. This includes: • Character creation • Core Mechanics • Skills, abilities, and magic • Basics of gameplay These rules give you the essentials of how to play using the system and provide a framework for adapting it for use in your own settings and games. There are some areas that differ across Bleak Forest games, depending on the modular rules that are being used, with character advancement being amongst them. The core rules as presented here offer a basic advancement system for use. The other modular systems for the Bleak Forest Rules are: • Escape the Bleak Forest: a solo character funnel that gamifies the character creation process. Through death and recursion, you will eventually discover your truest self and exit the Bleak Forest. • Beyond the Bleak Forest is a doomed, haunted world where you must prevent the rise of the Six Avatars of corruption known as the Abrikoth. Carrying over from Beyond the Bleak Forest, you further refine your abilities and manifest your fate. Beyond the Bleak Forest can be a solo, co-op, or GM-led game. These modular systems are not required to play the game; they offer an expansion on the core game mechanics and add setting and a bit of flavour. Additionally, there are a lot of subsystems and rules for things like durability, availability, consumables, etc. I added these to give a verisimilitude, a feel of impending doom, and ultimately, I wanted even the little choices to be consequential. Use them or not, but I felt that they added to the ominous feel of the game.

The Core Mechanic

Whenever a player attempts an action with a risk of failure, they roll a 20-sided die (d20) and add their relevant attribute modifier. If the final total equals or exceeds the Difficulty Class (DC) set by the Guide, the action succeeds.

Heroic Attributes

Characters are tracked using three primary attributes that quantify their physical and mental prowess.

Attribute Description Common Check
Corpus Corpus represents your physical body. It affects how well you can lift things, climb, dodge, and how well you endure hardship.
Anima Anima represents your mind. It reflects your emotions, determination, and how you interact with others. Anima helps with tasks like convincing people, spotting danger, or noticing hidden things.
Spiritus Spiritus represents your inner spirit or essence. It’s important for things like casting spells, empowering sigils, and making deals with magical beings.
